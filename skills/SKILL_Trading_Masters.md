## MASTER 1 — JESSE LIVERMORE

**Core:** Patience creates wealth.

**Psychology:**

- Big money comes from waiting — not from trading frequently
- Do not force trades — if no setup, no trade
- Follow price action, not opinions or news

**Strategy:**

- Identify the major trend first — trade WITH it, not against it
- Let winners run — never cut a winning trade early
- Cut losers quickly — no exceptions, no hope

**Mindset:**

- The market is always right — you are never right, the market is
- Avoid ego — never try to prove you are right
- Avoid revenge trading — one loss does not need to be recovered immediately

**Applied to LRS:**
→ Wait for the liquidity sweep. Do not enter early.
→ Once in a winning trade, let it reach the TP target.
→ When SL is hit, accept it. Monitor. Do not revenge trade.

## MASTER 2 — MARK DOUGLAS

**Core:** Consistency comes from psychology, not strategy.

**Psychology:**

- Every trade has an uncertain outcome — accept this before entering
- Think in probabilities — one loss means nothing over 50+ trades
- Accept the loss BEFORE you enter — mentally commit to the risk

**Strategy:**

- Execute the plan regardless of how you feel
- Focus on the process (did I follow my rules?) not the outcome (did I win?)
- A losing trade that followed all rules = a GOOD trade

**Mindset:**

- Fear and greed are the real enemies — not the market
- Confidence comes from discipline, not from recent wins
- Never judge a strategy by 3 trades — judge by 50+ trades

**Applied to LRS:**
→ Before entering: accept that this trade may lose $35.
→ After losing: grade yourself on execution quality, not P/L.
→ Never increase lot size after a win (overconfidence) or after a loss (revenge).

## MASTER 3 — MARKET WIZARDS (Jack Schwager)

**Core:** There is no holy grail. Risk management is universal.

**Psychology:**

- All great traders think differently — but ALL manage risk the same way
- Losses are business expenses — not failures, not punishments

**Strategy:**

- Protect capital first — always, every day, every trade
- Wait for high-probability opportunities — most days, do nothing
- The best traders pass on 90% of setups

**Mindset:**

- Discipline beats intelligence — a disciplined average trader beats an undisciplined genius
- The edge is in risk management, not in being right

**Applied to LRS:**
→ $35 SL is non-negotiable — this IS the risk management.
→ 3 trades maximum per day — this IS the discipline.
→ Daily $50 loss limit — this IS capital protection.

## MASTER 4 — GEORGE SOROS

**Core:** Macro forces move markets.

**Psychology:**

- Stay flexible — change your bias when facts change
- Do not fall in love with a position
- Being wrong and losing little = professional. Being wrong and losing big = amateur.

**Strategy for XAUUSD:**

- Monitor: US interest rates (Fed decisions)
- Monitor: US Dollar strength (DXY index)
- Monitor: Inflation data (CPI, PCE)
- Monitor: Geopolitical risk (wars, sanctions, crises)
- Build positions when evidence strengthens — not on prediction

**Gold macro rules:**

- Dollar UP → Gold DOWN (usually)
- Dollar DOWN → Gold UP (usually)
- War/crisis → Gold UP (safe haven demand)
- Rate cuts expected → Gold UP
- Rate hikes expected → Gold DOWN

**Applied to LRS:**
→ Check DXY and macro context before every session.
→ If macro contradicts your technical setup — reduce confidence to below 90% → skip trade.
→ If macro confirms your technical setup — confidence increases → valid entry.

## MASTER 5 — PAUL TUDOR JONES

**Core:** Defense before offense. Survivability is the key edge.

**Psychology:**

- Obsess over losses, not profits
- Ask before every trade: "What is the worst that can happen?"
- Never add to a losing position

**Strategy:**

- Preserve capital at all costs — a trader with capital always has another chance
- Increase position size ONLY when the market confirms your thesis
- 5:1 minimum reward-to-risk before entering

**Mindset:**

- Losers average losers — never add to a trade going against you
- The best trade is sometimes no trade

**Applied to LRS:**
→ Ask before every entry: "If this hits my SL of $35, can I absorb it mentally and financially?"
→ Never open a second trade in the same direction as a losing open trade.
→ The Hedge System activates only AFTER +$100 profit — never before.

## MASTER 6 — ED SEYKOTA

**Core:** Ride trends. Simplicity beats complexity.

**Psychology:**

- Traders get from markets exactly what they psychologically seek
- If you seek excitement → you will overtrade and lose
- If you seek process → you will find consistency

**Strategy:**

- Follow the trend — do not fight it
- Cut losses immediately — no delay, no hope
- Let profits grow — do not close early because of fear

**Mindset:**

- Simple rules consistently applied beat complex systems poorly applied
- "Win or lose, everybody gets what they want from the market"

**Applied to LRS:**
→ LRS is simple: sweep → confirm → enter. Do not complicate it.
→ If you are overtrading, ask yourself: am I seeking excitement or profit?
→ Trend on H4 must support your entry direction — never fight H4 trend.

-----

## MASTER 7 — RAY DALIO

**Core:** Reality over ego. Pain + Reflection = Progress.

**Psychology:**

- Seek opposing viewpoints — always ask "what if I am wrong?"
- Pain from a loss + honest reflection = improvement
- Ego is the biggest enemy of a trader

**Strategy:**

- Build systems, not opinions — your rules must be written, tested, followed
- Think in probabilities — no single trade defines you
- Radical transparency with yourself — journal every trade honestly

**Mindset:**

- Learn quickly when wrong — do not delay the lesson
- The best investors are humble — they know they can be wrong

**Applied to LRS:**
→ After every loss, ask: "Was this a system failure or an execution failure?"
→ Journal honestly — do not hide mistakes from your own journal
→ The Trading Journal Template exists for this exact reason
